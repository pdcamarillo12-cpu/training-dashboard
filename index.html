Universal rules
📈
Add weight only when you complete all reps with 1–2 reps left in the tank across every set. Two sessions at the top of the rep range = green light.
🔀
Two paths to progress: more weight or more sets/reps. Always increase volume before load — add sets before adding plates.
📝
Log every session. Date, exercise, sets × reps × weight. A notes app is enough. You cannot overload what you don't track.
⏱️
Rest periods matter. Hypertrophy block: 90–120 sec. Strength block: 2.5–4 min. Rushing rest kills strength gains.
📉
If performance drops two sessions in a row — drop load 10%, recover, climb again. That's not failure, that's the process.
🔄
After week 12, restart the cycle. New starting weights should be 5–10% higher than cycle one. That's how you compound progress.
